## 🪰 BioReservoir — a real fruit-fly brain you can ask a question

<a href="https://fly.igdigi.com"><img align="right" width="300" src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/igdigitallab/.github/main/profile/fly.gif" alt="A question going through all 165,122 neurons of the male fruit-fly connectome, live at fly.igdigi.com"></a>

In June 2026, Janelia published the complete wiring diagram of a male fruit fly's central nervous system. We put that brain on a public page.

Type a yes-or-no question: it becomes input on the fly's real taste, smell and hearing neurons, the whole brain runs for a quarter of a second, and a 3D fly turns toward YES or NO while every spike replays at its real position.

- **165,122 neurons · 6.2 million connections** — MaleCNS v1.0, CC BY 4.0
- **No chatbot in the loop** — the wiring decides
- Reproduces a published *Nature* (2024) result on the same brain; the failed check is published too
- Runs live on our own servers in Sacramento

**[▶ Ask the fly](https://fly.igdigi.com)** · [Code](https://github.com/igdigitallab/bioreservoir) · [Video](https://github.com/igdigitallab/bioreservoir/blob/main/docs/media/fly-24s.mp4) · [Numbers & sources](https://fly.igdigi.com/data)
